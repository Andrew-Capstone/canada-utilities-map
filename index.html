Drawn from 20–30 m elevation data, accurate to a few metres. Intervals under 5 m (15 ft) are smoothed estimates, not survey lines. Shown from zoom 12. For a clean background, use the Hillshade basemap; Esri's Topographic map has its own fixed contours.

Power infrastructure (OpenStreetMap)
Gas infrastructure (OpenStreetMap)

OpenStreetMap has no gas pressure or flow data for Ontario, so pipelines are split by type. Transmission lines carry the most gas.

Fibre & telecom

No public map of Canada's fibre trunk routes exists. Long-haul fibre largely follows the CN and CP mainlines, so those are shown as a stand-in, not actual fibre. OpenStreetMap telecom lines are mostly Nova Scotia and New Brunswick utility lines. Northwestel and N.W.T. routes are official (N.W.T. government). ISED flags communities with no fibre backbone nearby.
